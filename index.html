New York Bail Reform and Pretrial Detention Outcomes

Pythonpandasscikit-learnSQLDuckDB

With collaborator Hodan Aden, I analyzed over 1.3M New York DCJS pretrial case records from 2019 to 2024, merged with U.S. Census ACS county level poverty data, to test whether pretrial detention predicts conviction outcomes and how the state's 2020 bail reform changed that relationship.

  • Built a cleaned, structured database joining case, county, and poverty data
  • Validated results with VIF multicollinearity checks and weighted regression models
  • Found that poverty rate significantly predicts the gap between detention and conviction rates
