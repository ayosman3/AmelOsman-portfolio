About

CASE NO. 2026-01 Capstone · In progress

Pretrial Detention, Poverty, and the 2020 Bail Reform Law

DuckDBRegressionVIF DiagnosticsPolicy Data

DATA 510 capstone with a partner, supervised by faculty. Built a database from New York State's Supplemental Pretrial Release Data (1.3M+ case records, 2019–2024) combined with county-level poverty data across all 62 NY counties, to ask whether pretrial detention predicts conviction outcomes — and whether that relationship shifted after the 2020 bail reform law, or varies by county poverty level.

Poverty rate is a significant predictor of the detention–conviction gap (β = 0.85, p < 0.001, R² = 15%)
